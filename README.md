Scripts for analyses used in Assis et al. 2025, Evolution https://doi.org/10.1093/evolut/qpaf056    
In this study, we first determine that the trade-off between respiration and water loss in Plethodon metcalfi is malleable throughout climatic variation rather than permanent (Figure 1). Similarly, acclimation to standardized conditions mitigates the trade-off (Figure 2).    
Afterwards, we identify genetic regulatory processes that act both drive the trade-off (Figure 3) and mitigate the trade-off (Figure 4).
